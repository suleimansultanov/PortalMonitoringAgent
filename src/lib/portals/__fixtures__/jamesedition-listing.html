The BERTI AND SONS real estate group offers for sale, in a sought-after residential area of Saint-Tropez, this property boasting a prime location near the village and beaches, with pleasant sea views. Situated on a level plot of 2,666 sqm, the villa offers a fluid layout and generous living spaces. The reception areas include an entrance hall, a spacious living room, a lounge, a dining room, and a separate kitchen. Four bedrooms, several shower rooms and bathrooms, as well as a dressing room, complete the ground floor. The lower level features a large multipurpose space, two additional bedrooms with en-suite shower rooms, significantly increasing the accommodation capacity. A separate apartment comprising a living room with a kitchen, a bedroom, and a shower room provides independent living space. Outside, the perfectly level landscaped garden showcases a tiled infinity pool, harmoniously integrated into the overall design. The terraces extend the living spaces naturally and offer sea views. Garage and pool equipment room. A rare property in this area.
